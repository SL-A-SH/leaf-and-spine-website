Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Leaf & Spine was built on a simple idea: your reading life belongs to you. No account is required, and no personal information is collected. This page describes the little we do handle, and why.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous user IDs (Firebase Authentication)
  • Purchase history (stored locally and in Firebase)
  • Device identifiers for push notifications (Expo Push Token)
  • Crash reports, if you leave them enabled (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version)

How We Use Information

  • To provide in-app purchase functionality
  • To prevent fraud and validate purchases

Data Storage

  • Purchase data is stored securely in Firebase
  • User IDs are anonymized
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or the contents of your library. Books, highlights, notes and reading progress stay on your device.

Push Notifications

  • We may send push notifications to remind you to use the app
  • We collect a device identifier (push token) to deliver these notifications
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time
  • Push tokens are sent to Expo Push Service for delivery only

Crash Reports

When the app closes unexpectedly, it can send a report so the fault can be found and fixed. This is on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports.


A crash report contains:

  • The technical stack trace of the failure
  • Your device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and app version
  • Device characteristics such as screen size, available memory and storage, battery level, timezone and language
  • The list of Android permissions the app has been granted
  • A randomly generated identifier for your installation of the app, so repeat reports can be recognised as coming from one device. It resets if you reinstall and is not linked to you, your device's hardware, or any account
  • Which screen you were on, and the type of book open (PDF, EPUB, or audiobook)
  • Country and region derived by our crash reporting provider. Your IP address is not stored

A crash report never contains:

  • The titles, authors, contents, or file names of your books
  • Your highlights, notes, drawings, or reading progress
  • Your name, email address, or account details

Crash reports are processed by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., United States) and are not used for advertising or analytics.

Your library

Your books, highlights, notes, drawings and reading progress are stored on your device and are never sent to us. If you choose to back up, they are copied to your own Google Drive account, as described below.

Google Drive Backup

Backup and restore is optional. Nothing is sent to Google Drive unless you start a backup or restore yourself.


When you sign in with Google:

  • Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app to complete sign-in. These stay on your device and are not sent to us
  • The app asks only for access to files it creates in your Drive. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive

What a backup contains:

  • A backup file (leaf_and_spine_backup.json) with your library details, highlights, notes, drawings, reading progress, shelves and reading settings, plus your device's platform and model so you can tell backups apart
  • If you choose to include them, copies of your book files, kept in a folder named "Leaf & Spine Books"

How it is handled:

  • Backups go directly from your device to your Google Drive. They never pass through or are stored on our servers, and we cannot read them
  • Your Google data is used only to back up and restore your library. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models
  • You can delete your backup at any time by removing those files from your Google Drive
  • You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions

Leaf & Spine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

iCloud Backup

On iPhone and iPad you can back up to iCloud Drive instead of Google Drive. It is optional, and nothing is saved there unless you start a backup yourself.

  • The backup uses the Apple ID already signed in on your device. The app does not see your Apple ID, name or email address
  • A backup contains the same things as a Google Drive backup: the leaf_and_spine_backup.json file, and copies of your book files if you choose to include them
  • Files are saved in a "Leaf & Spine" folder in your iCloud Drive, which you can see and delete in the Files app
  • Apple syncs the files between your devices. They never pass through or are stored on our servers, and we cannot read them. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive (only if you use backup)
  • Apple iCloud Drive (only if you back up to iCloud)
  • Google Play In-App Billing
  • Apple App Store In-App Purchase
  • Sentry

Purchases

I bought Premium but the app doesn’t show it

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. If it still doesn’t appear, write to us with the date of purchase; please don’t send card details.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the App Store and Google Play, so refunds are requested from them: on iPhone and iPad at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android from your order history in Google Play.

Adding books

Use Add books to pick files, scan a folder, download free books from Discover, or connect your own server. The app opens EPUB and PDF books, CBZ and CBR comics, and audiobooks. MOBI and AZW3 are not supported; convert them to EPUB first, for example with Calibre.

A book won’t open

The file may be damaged or incomplete, often from an interrupted download. Try opening it on a computer; if it fails there too, download it again. If it opens elsewhere but not in Leaf & Spine, write to us and say where the book came from.

Backups

With Premium you can back up your library to your own Google Drive, or to iCloud Drive on iPhone and iPad, from Settings. A backup holds your library details, highlights, notes, drawings and reading progress, and your book files if you choose to include them. To move to a new phone, install the app, sign in to the same account, and restore.

Syncing your reading

Settings › Sync progress with my servers keeps your place in step with Audiobookshelf, Komga, Kavita and KOReader. Automatic sync is off until you turn it on; Sync now works either way.

KOReader doesn’t pick up my book

KOReader recognises a book by its file, so both devices need the same file: one downloaded from the same server, or copied across unchanged. An EPUB starts syncing after it has been opened in Leaf & Spine once, and opens at the start of the chapter KOReader was in.

A server says it couldn’t be reached

Check the server is switched on and that your phone is on the same network. Away from home, use the server’s https address.

Your data

Your books, highlights and notes stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them; backups in your Google Drive or iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them. Crash reports can be turned off in Settings › Privacy. The privacy policy has the details.
